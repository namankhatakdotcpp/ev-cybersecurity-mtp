# Gate 6 (reduced) and S5 report

Tables: `results/gate6_tables.md` (generated by `backend/pipeline/gate6/descriptive_b0.py`; config `configs/gate6_descriptive.yaml`, committed before the run). Everything here is exploratory and descriptive; no hypothesis test was run in the Gate 6 part.

## Part 1. Reduced Gate 6: descriptive coefficients and error analysis for the exposure-only reference B0

**Protocol reduction (D072).** Protocol v1 allowed interpretability (SHAP, permutation importance) for the selected model. No model beat B0 in Gate 3, and the SLMs are a separate track, so SHAP or permutation importance would describe noise. Instead: coefficients of B0 and an error analysis of its Gate 3 out-of-fold predictions by number of attacked kiosks, trial index and collection period (plus number of available kiosks and incident mode).

**Set.** 492 rows, 112 participants, 220 events (the T1 primary set: station visits where the participant charged and the station had both an attacked and a safe available kiosk). B0 = exposure offset logit(p_exp) + n_available + incident_mode + period dummies.

**Coefficients.** All covariate coefficients are indistinguishable from zero. Unpenalised, with participant-clustered intervals: n_available OR 1.01 [0.66, 1.46], incident_mode 0.92 [0.62, 1.39], June 1.12 [0.51, 2.20], July-August 1.18 [0.79, 1.78] (reference April). The ridge fit at the selected C = 0.01 shrinks them to |coef| <= 0.03. B0's predictive content is therefore almost entirely the exposure offset. With the exposure entered as a free covariate instead of an offset, its slope is 1.10 [0.68, 1.52], i.e. consistent with choice following the proportion of attacked kiosks.

**Error analysis (Gate 3 out-of-fold B0).**
- Across the primary set the observed attacked-choice rate is 0.447, B0 predicts 0.447, and exposure alone would predict 0.493. Observed minus exposure is -0.046 [-0.088, -0.004]: participants chose an attacked kiosk about 5 points less often than a random pick among the available kiosks. This is the only sign of behaviour beyond exposure in this part; it is small, it is across all rows, and its mechanism is not identified (visible cues such as image type, or position, or a chance effect).
- By number of attacked kiosks: 1 of 2/3 attacked: observed 0.375 vs exposure 0.428 (-0.052 [-0.101, -0.003]); 2 attacked: 0.637 vs 0.667 (-0.030 [-0.124, +0.055]). By exposure cell: 1 of 2 0.450 vs 0.500; 1 of 3 0.277 vs 0.333; 2 of 3 0.637 vs 0.667. All cells lie below the random-pick rate and all intervals except those listed include zero.
- By trial index (1-5, 6-10, 11-15, 16-20): observed minus B0 is -0.028, +0.058, -0.037, +0.080 with intervals spanning zero (16-20 has 15 rows and 15 participants). No monotone trend.
- By collection period: April 0.427 vs exposure 0.494 (-0.067 [-0.126, -0.006]); June 0.455 (44 rows); July-August 0.460 (-0.034, interval spans zero). B0's calibration-in-the-large is within +/-0.02 in every period.
- Calibration-in-the-large of B0 is within bootstrap noise in every stratum (all obs-minus-B0 intervals include zero). Within-stratum AUROC values (0.42 to 0.66) are not interpretable: B0 varies little inside a stratum, and intervals were not computed for them.

## Critical review

- **What we learned.** B0 is an exposure model. Its covariates do nothing, its calibration is fine everywhere we can look, and the one thing the data show beyond exposure is a modest overall tendency to choose attacked kiosks a little less than random.
- **What failed or is uncertain.** Strata are small (trial 16-20 has 15 rows; June 44 rows); many strata were looked at without multiplicity control, so the three intervals that exclude zero (all rows, one attacked kiosk, April) must not be treated as findings; they are one signal seen at three levels of the same data.
- **Defensible.** B0's coefficients are null; the offset slope is consistent with 1; B0 is calibrated overall and by stratum within noise.
- **Not defensible.** Any statement that participants "detected" attacks; any trend over trials or periods; any use of the within-stratum AUROC.
- **Do not test next.** SHAP or permutation importance on Gate 3 models; further strata or cut-points on these rows.
